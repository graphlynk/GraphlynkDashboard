# Graphlynk — Business Model

**Prepared for Investor Review**
*Date: May 2026*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Graphlynk** is an AI-powered SEO & Visibility intelligence platform that helps businesses, creators, and multi-entity organizations monitor, optimize, and grow their digital presence across search engines, generative AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini), and social discovery surfaces.

Built with a **Vision UI** premium aesthetic, Graphlynk combines a real-time visibility dashboard, a "Panel Readiness" optimization engine, and an admin console for agencies and enterprise teams managing multiple brands or clients.

**Mission:** Make every business discoverable everywhere it matters — from Google to AI assistants — through a single, intelligent control panel.

---

## 2. The Problem

- **Fragmented visibility:** Brands now compete for attention across Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn. There is no unified view.
- **AI search is rewriting SEO:** ~40% of informational queries are now answered by generative AI, often without click-through. Most SMBs have no insight into how (or whether) AI surfaces mention them.
- **Agencies struggle to scale:** Managing SEO and visibility for dozens of clients requires switching between 6–10 disconnected tools.
- **Knowledge Panel & entity readiness is invisible:** Most businesses don't know if Google, Bing, or AI engines have a complete, accurate "knowledge graph entity" for them.

---

## 3. The Solution

Graphlynk delivers a **single dashboard** with:

1. **SEO & Visibility Overview** — real-time rankings, AI mentions, branded search trends.
2. **Panel Readiness Score** — proprietary score measuring how "ready" a business is to appear as a knowledge entity in Google, Bing, and AI answer engines.
3. **Multi-Entity Tabs** — manage multiple brands, locations, or client accounts in one workspace.
4. **Profile & Links Optimizer** — guided workflows to claim, verify, and enrich digital profiles.
5. **AI Assistant Monitoring** — track how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude describe your brand.
6. **Admin Console** — role-based access, white-labeling, and team collaboration for agencies and enterprises.

## 6. Revenue Streams

1. **SaaS Subscriptions** (primary, ~85% of revenue) — monthly & annual recurring.
2. **Done-For-You Services** (~10%) — Panel Readiness optimization, profile claiming, content sprints. High-margin add-on, also acts as upsell pipeline.
3. **API & Data Licensing** (~3%) — visibility data feeds for agencies and martech partners.
4. **Marketplace / Affiliate** (~2%) — integrations with directory services, schema generators, AI content tools (rev share).
